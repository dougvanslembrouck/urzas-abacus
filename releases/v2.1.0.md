# v2.1.0: Live Commander Search

The commander picker now searches **Scryfall live**, so every new commander shows up as soon as Scryfall lists it. No more outdated list, and nothing to update by hand.

**▶ Play it:** https://dougvanslembrouck.github.io/nja-edh-pod-life-tracker/

## 🖖 New commanders, right away
The Star Trek and Star Trek Commander sets are in: Kirk, Picard, Spock, Janeway, Seven of Nine, Locutus and the rest. That's about 3,750 commanders, up from 3,497, and future sets arrive automatically.

## 🎨 Artist credit, kept
Every pick still uses the commander's newest standard printing and credits its artist on the card. Two refinements:
- When a set has both a regular and a borderless printing of a commander, the regular one wins. Kirk, Commanding Officer shows Cliff Nielsen's art, not the borderless alternate.
- Double-faced commanders credit the artist of the face shown.

## ⚡ Feels instant
- Search starts after 2 letters and a short pause in typing, with "Searching Scryfall…" while it works.
- Picking shows the name right away; art and artist follow a moment later.
- Picks are saved with their art and artist, so they come back even offline.

## 🧹 Smaller download
The bundled 300 KB commander list and its updater tool are gone.

## Upgrading from v2.0
Replace the folder. Commanders you picked in v2.0 are upgraded automatically the next time you open the app online.

**Note:** commander search now needs internet. Everything else (life, damage, tax, monarch, history) still works offline.
