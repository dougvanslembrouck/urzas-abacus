v2.0.0 NJA EDH Pod Life Tracker · November 3, 2026

Not Just Another Life Tracker

A rebuild from the ground up, with a table layout that's faster to use mid-game. Your match history and commander picks carry over.

Play v2.0

Tap to change life

The round buttons are gone. The whole life area is now the button.

  • Tap anywhere on the right half to gain 1, the left half to lose 1, including right on the number
  • Faint − and + marks show which side is which
  • Rotated seats use the player's own left and right
  • The life total is 20% bigger and scales to fit narrow cards

Your commander's name on your seat

Pick a commander and your seat takes its name everywhere it appears.

  • On your own card, and in every opponent's commander damage rows
  • Back to the land background? Your old name returns
  • A name you typed yourself is kept
  • Long names end in "…" when space is tight

Controls in the center

2P / 3P / 4P, Reset, Log Game and History now sit in the exact center of the table, where every player can reach them.

  • Cards and the commander search leave room, so nothing gets covered
  • More compact on phones, so all six buttons fit
  • Works in 2, 3 and 4 player layouts

Upgrading from v1.x

Replace the whole folder with this release. The old root-level commanders.js and land images are no longer used. Match history and commander picks are kept automatically on the same device and browser.
