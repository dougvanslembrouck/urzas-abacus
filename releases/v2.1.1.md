# v2.1.1: Urza's Abacus

The life tracker has a new name, **Urza's Abacus**, three ways to seat four players, and every seat now shows its commander's colors.

**▶ Play it:** https://dougvanslembrouck.github.io/urzas-abacus/app/

**Website:** https://dougvanslembrouck.github.io/urzas-abacus/

## 🪑 Seat four players your way
Tap **4P** and pick a layout to match your table:
- **Ends:** two players at each end, as before.
- **Sides:** two players along each long side of the tablet.
- **Around:** one player on each side, every seat the same size.

The tablet remembers your choice. Tap **4P** again during a game to switch.

## 📱 Made for tablets
Urza's Abacus is now designed for a tablet lying flat in portrait. Phones still open it, but layouts are tuned for tablets only.

## 🎨 Color identity on every seat
Pick a commander and its color identity appears as mana symbols right under the commander tax counter, in the order printed on cards: {R}{W} for Boros, {W}{U}{B} for Esper, {C} for colorless. The pips show up the moment you pick, before the art finishes loading. The commander search uses the same symbols.

## 🧮 Meet Urza's Abacus
Urza was Magic's greatest artificer, and an abacus is the oldest counting machine there is. Same app, same features, a name that fits. It also gets an app icon, and "Add to Home Screen" now labels it *Urza's Abacus*.

## 🧹 A tidier download
Leftover v1 files (the old commander list, duplicate land images, old screenshots and zips) are gone, and the repository has bug report and feature request forms.

## Upgrading from v2.1.0
Replace the folder. Your match history and commander picks carry over, since the saved data hasn't changed. Commanders picked in v2.1.0 already have their colors saved, so their pips appear right away.
