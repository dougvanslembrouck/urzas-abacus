# v2.0.0: Not Just Another Life Tracker

A rebuild from the ground up, with a table layout that's faster to use mid-game. Your match history and commander picks carry over.

**▶ Play it:** https://dougvanslembrouck.github.io/nja-edh-pod-life-tracker/

## ✋ Tap to change life
The round buttons are gone. **Tap anywhere on the right half** of your life area to gain 1, and **the left half** to lose 1, including right on the number. Faint − and + marks show the sides, and rotated seats use the player's own left and right. The life total is also **20% bigger**.

## 🏷️ Your commander's name on your seat
Pick a commander and your seat takes its name, on your card and in every opponent's commander damage rows. Go back to the land background and your old name returns.

## 🎯 Controls in the center
2P / 3P / 4P, Reset, Log Game, and History now sit in the **exact center of the table**, where every player can reach them. Cards and the commander search leave room so nothing gets covered. On phones the bar is more compact, so all six buttons fit.

## 🏗️ Rebuilt as Model–View–Controller
The single 1,250-line file is now an organized project:
- `js/models/` for data and rules, `js/views/` for drawing, `js/controllers/` connecting them
- `css/`, `data/`, and `images/` folders
- `js/config.js` holds starting life, lethal damage, tax step, and seat names in one place

It still needs no install or build step: double-click `index.html` and play.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **Winner detection:** an eliminated player with a high life total no longer blocks the win.
- **Phone 4P:** the life total no longer spills off the card, and the control bar no longer runs off the screen.
- Long commander names end in "…" instead of being cut off mid-letter.

## 📚 New docs
A full README with a how-to-play guide, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, issue templates, and an MIT license.

## Upgrading from v1.x
Replace the whole folder with this release. The old root-level `commanders.js` and land PNGs are no longer used. Match history and commander picks are kept automatically on the same device and browser.

Full details: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/dougvanslembrouck/nja-edh-pod-life-tracker/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
