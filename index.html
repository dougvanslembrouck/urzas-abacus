Seat everyone the way you actually sit

Lay the tablet flat in portrait and pick 2, 3 or 4 players from the bar in the center. Every card turns to face its player, so nobody reads their life upside down. Four players get three layouts to match your table.

Tap 4P once to choose a layout. The tablet remembers your pick, and tapping 4P again mid-game lets you switch.

Life and commander damage, in one tap

The whole life area is the button: tap the right half to gain 1 and the left half to lose 1. Try it on the card here.

  • Commander damage comes off life too. Add 5 from an opponent's commander and the player loses 5 life. Remove it and they get it back.
  • 21 turns the row red. A pulsing warning means someone has taken lethal damage from one commander.
  • Everyone starts at 40. Life can go below zero, and fast tapping never zooms the page.
  • I'm Dead greys out a knocked-out player's card. It becomes Undo in case of a mis-tap.

Your commander, on your seat

Tap Choose commander, type a few letters, and pick. The search covers every commander-legal card on Scryfall, so new sets appear the day Scryfall lists them.

  • The card's art becomes your background, taken from its newest standard printing, with the artist credited on your card.
  • Your seat is renamed to your commander, on your card and in every opponent's damage rows.
  • Color identity shows as mana pips under the tax counter, in the order printed on cards. Boros reads R W, colorless shows C.
  • Remembered on the tablet, so next game your commanders are already in place, even without internet.
  • Or play on a land. Each seat can show any of the five basic lands, Island included, and Clear Commanders sets the whole table back to lands in one tap.
  • Tap the book for Lore. The card flips over to show the AlphaCryptids of its land: who they are and where their legends come from.

The rest of the table, tracked

The small things a pod forgets by turn eight, kept on each player's card.

TAX −4+

Commander tax

Counts in steps of 2, so the number is exactly the extra mana your next cast costs. It turns amber once you've paid any.

The monarch

Tap the crown to crown a player, and their card glows gold. Only one monarch at a time, and the crown leaves with an eliminated player.

I'm DeadbecomesUndo

Eliminations

A knocked-out player's card dims and locks. When the game ends, Reset sets life back to 40 and keeps everyone's names and commanders.

Every game, on the record

Tap Log Game when a game ends. History keeps every player's name, commander, final life, tax and the monarch, and marks the winner.

  • The winner is worked out for you: the last player standing, or the survivors with the most life. Ties share the win.
  • Export and import history as a JSON file to back it up or move it to another tablet.
  • Stays on your tablet. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Ready in a minute

There's nothing to download or sign up for. Urza's Abacus runs in your tablet's browser.

  1. Open it on your tablet

    Go to the app on the iPad or Android tablet you'll put on the table, held in portrait.

  2. Add it to your home screen

    It then opens full screen, like an app.

    iPad: Share, then Add to Home Screen Android: ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen

  3. Choose players and commanders

    Tap 2P, 3P or 4P and pick a layout. Each player taps Choose commander on their own card.

Questions

Does it work on a phone?

Urza's Abacus is built for tablets in portrait: iPad, Android tablets and similar. It opens on a phone or computer, but the layouts are designed for a tablet-sized screen lying flat between players.

Does it need internet?

Only to search for commanders and load their art and mana symbols, which come from Scryfall. Life, commander damage, tax, the monarch and match history all work offline, and commanders you've already picked keep their names.

Where is my data kept?

The app keeps everything in your tablet's browser. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or cookies. Use Export JSON to back up your match history or move it to another tablet. The only thing this website collects is what you type into the AlphaCryptids sign-up form, which goes to a Google Form we manage and is used only for your pack and Urza's Abacus news.

How do I get the free AlphaCryptids lands?

Fill in the sign-up form with your name and email, and choose whether to pick your pack up at MagicCon Detroit 2027 or have it mailed to you. We'll email you with the details.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free and open source under the MIT license. You can also host your own copy on GitHub Pages; the README on GitHub shows how.

Can it track partners, poison or the initiative?

Not yet. Each player has one commander, and poison, energy, experience and the initiative aren't tracked. They're on the roadmap, and ideas are welcome as issues on GitHub.

Is a game saved if I refresh?

Not yet. Refreshing mid-game resets life, damage, tax and the monarch. Logged games and each seat's commander are kept.
